## Design Context

### Users
Personal music listeners using Folia with a playback module and a small companion remote window. They need glanceable lyrics, buffering feedback and the actual audio transfer rate.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, unobtrusive. Keep attention on the music and lyrics.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing compact remote-window typography, themes and idle sleeve presentation. This task adds feedback, not a redesign.

### Design Principles

- Keep transfer feedback visible without covering lyrics or hiding behind idle controls.
- Reuse existing colors, icons and unit formatting.
- Freeze progress while waiting; make cancellation understandable and available.
- Respect reduced motion and avoid announcing every speed update.
